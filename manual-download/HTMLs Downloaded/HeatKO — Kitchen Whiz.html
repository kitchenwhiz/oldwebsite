What we do

HEATKO is a premier manufacturer of high-performance commercial kitchen equipment, engineered to bridge the gap between industrial power and culinary finesse. Specializing in heavy-duty tools—from Dimsum Steamers and Robata Grills to IR Strip Heaters—our equipment is designed to meet the rigorous demands of professional kitchens while delivering unparalleled heat control and energy efficiency. By combining advanced technology with sleek, functional craftsmanship, HEATKO minimizes electrical load and environmental impact without compromising on the durability chefs rely on. At HEATKO, we don't just provide kitchen hardware; we provide the precision, safety, and sustainable design required to power the future of the global food industry.
